## CLAIM claim:1e61c7154b59a8af-586c0b4788cea75b
Unacceptable risk AI practices are prohibited and include: subliminal techniques causing harm; exploiting vulnerabilities due to age, disability, or social/economic situation; social scoring; predictive policing based on profiling; untargeted scraping of facial images; emotion inference in workplace/education; biometric categorization of sensitive characteristics; real-time remote biometric identification for law enforcement (with narrow exceptions).
Conditions: none
Validity: SUPPORTED

## CLAIM claim:1e61c7154b59a8af-a48a3f1ae8ac6838
Prohibitions on unacceptable risk practices take effect on 2 February 2025.
Conditions: none
Validity: SUPPORTED

## CLAIM claim:1e61c7154b59a8af-71f81474af0d6bf3
For other infringements, penalties are up to €15 million or 3% of total worldwide annual turnover.
Conditions: none
Validity: SUPPORTED

## CLAIM claim:1e61c7154b59a8af-9a9c2a479295d164

Conditions: none
Validity: SUPPORTED
