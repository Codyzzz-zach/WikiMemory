### Industry Context

Meta is not the only company affected by the Schrems II ruling. The fundamental question of whether US surveillance law provides "essentially equivalent" protection to EU law affects all transatlantic data transfers, not just Meta's. The decision came amid ongoing negotiations for a new EU-US Data Privacy Framework (subsequently adopted as an adequacy decision in July 2023).

According to enforcementtracker.com, this was the largest GDPR fine ever imposed.
